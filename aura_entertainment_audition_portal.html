NEED TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Who can apply?

Applicants of all genders and nationalities may apply. If you are under 18, parent or legal guardian consent is required. AURA may publish additional eligibility requirements for specific audition rounds.

Which categories can I choose?

Vocal, Rap, Dance, Acting, Modeling and Special Talent. Choose the category that best showcases your strongest ability.

What should I upload?

Upload a recent profile photo and a clear audition video. Keep your face visible, use good lighting and make sure the performance can be heard or seen clearly. Do not upload copyrighted material unless you have permission to use it.

Will everyone receive a response?

AURA will contact shortlisted applicants using the email address supplied. Please check your inbox and spam folder. Submission does not guarantee selection or a trainee contract.

Is there an application fee?

AURA does not request payment through this demo portal. Official fee and privacy policies must be published by AURA before this portal is used for live applications.
